DESIGN NOTES

A visual prototype of the investment platform

Twenty-five screens from your specification, plus two superseded ones kept for reference. The investor path — landing through registration, login and dashboard — is clickable: filters, the calculator, comparison selection and the score editor all respond. The developer side covers sign-up, the portal, project and unit management with price and availability edits, and gated leads; admin adds an approvals queue that every listing change and investor introduction passes through. Back-office and legal screens are laid out but static; the two legal pages carry draft text for review. Use the rail on the left to jump, or the buttons inside each screen to follow the flow as an investor would.

CLIENT / SITE

Light summer palette: near-white sky ground, clear blue for actions, deep blue-teal for headings, sunny yellow as the single accent.

INVESTOR / DEVELOPER

One step deeper and cooler than the public site. Same structure, so a signed-in user always knows which side of the platform they are on without it looking like a separate product.

ADMIN

The most muted of the three, and still light — not a dark console. Review queues are read for hours, so the back office matches the product rather than fighting it; the accent marks only decisions.
