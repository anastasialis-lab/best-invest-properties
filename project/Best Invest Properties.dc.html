DESIGN NOTES

A visual prototype of the investment platform

Nineteen screens from your specification. The investor path — landing through registration, login and dashboard — is clickable: filters, the calculator, comparison selection and the score editor all respond. Developer, back-office and legal screens are laid out but static; the two legal pages carry draft text for review. Use the rail on the left to jump, or the buttons inside each screen to follow the flow as an investor would.

PALETTE

Navy and gold taken from your logo. Warm paper ground instead of white, so financial tables read as documents rather than dashboards.
